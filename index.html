Eigenverse

Explore how eigenvalues and eigenvectors power web search, AI, vibrations, and quantum physics.

Core concept

An eigenvector \(\mathbf{v}\) of a matrix \(A\) satisfies \(A\mathbf{v} = \lambda \mathbf{v}\), where \(\lambda\) is the eigenvalue.

Matrix controller

Move sliders to change the matrix. Blue: original basis, Cyan: transformed basis, Purple/Pink: eigenvectors.

Real‑world applications

Google PageRank

Eigenvectors of a huge link matrix give each page an importance score used in ranking search results.

PCA & AI

Principal Component Analysis uses eigenvectors of the covariance matrix to find main directions of variance for compression and noise reduction in data.

Vibrating structures

Bridges and buildings have natural modes of vibration described by eigenvalues and eigenvectors of stiffness and mass matrices.

Quantum energy levels

In quantum mechanics, allowed energy levels are eigenvalues of the Hamiltonian operator, and eigenvectors describe the corresponding states.

Mechanical & civil Natural frequencies and mode shapes of structures from eigenvalue problems.
