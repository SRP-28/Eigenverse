Eigenverse

Explore how eigenvalues and eigenvectors power search engines, AI, vibrations, and quantum worlds.

Core concept

An eigenvector \( \mathbf{v} \) of a matrix \( A \) satisfies \( A\mathbf{v} = \lambda \mathbf{v} \), where \( \lambda \) is the eigenvalue.

Matrix controller

Blue: original vector, Red: transformed vector, Green: approximate eigenvector.

Real‑world applications

Google PageRank

Eigenvectors of the web link matrix give an importance score for each page, used in ranking search results.

PCA & AI

Principal Component Analysis uses eigenvectors of the covariance matrix to compress data and reduce noise in ML.

Vibrations

Bridges and buildings have natural vibration modes defined by eigenvalues and eigenvectors of stiffness matrices.

Quantum states

Eigenvalues of the Hamiltonian correspond to allowed energy levels; eigenvectors describe quantum states.
