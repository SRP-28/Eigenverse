Welcome

Eigenvalues and eigenvectors are fundamental concepts in linear algebra with numerous real-world applications.
This website introduces these concepts and explores where they are applied in science and engineering.

Basics

An eigenvector \\(\mathbf{v}\\) of a matrix \\(A\\) satisfies \\(A\mathbf{v} = \lambda \mathbf{v}\\), where \\(\lambda\\) is the eigenvalue.

Example matrix and vector:

\\[
    A = \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 1 & 2 \end{bmatrix},\quad \mathbf{v} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}
    \\]

Blue vector is original; red vector is \\(A \mathbf{v}\\).

Applications

Google PageRank

Eigenvectors determine the importance of web pages in Google's ranking algorithm.

Mechanical Vibrations

Modes of vibration in structures arise from eigenvalues and eigenvectors of system matrices.

Principal Component Analysis (PCA)

Eigenvectors identify main directions of variance in data for dimensionality reduction in machine learning.

Quantum Mechanics

Energy levels of atoms correspond to eigenvalues of Hamiltonian operators.
