Eigenverse

Explore how eigenvalues and eigenvectors power web search, AI, vibrations, and quantum physics.

Core concept

An eigenvector \(\mathbf{v}\) of a matrix \(A\) satisfies \(A\mathbf{v} = \lambda \mathbf{v}\), where \(\lambda\) is the eigenvalue.

Matrix controller

Move sliders to change the matrix. Blue: original vector, Red: \(A\mathbf{v}\), Green: approximate eigenvector.

Real‑world applications

Google PageRank

Eigenvectors of a huge link matrix give each page an importance score used in ranking search results.[web:1][web:14]

PCA & AI

Principal Component Analysis uses eigenvectors of the covariance matrix to find main directions of variance for compression and noise reduction in data.[web:1][web:8]

Vibrating structures

Bridges and buildings have natural modes of vibration described by eigenvalues and eigenvectors of stiffness and mass matrices.[web:1][web:10][web:11]

Quantum energy levels

In quantum mechanics, allowed energy levels are eigenvalues of the Hamiltonian operator, and eigenvectors describe the corresponding states.[web:3][web:8]

Where eigenvalues appear

Field Role of eigenvalues/eigenvectors
Web search Importance score of pages in the link network (PageRank vector).[web:1][web:14][web:16]
Data science (PCA) Principal components capture directions of maximum variance for dimensionality reduction.[web:1][web:8]
Mechanical & civil Natural frequencies and mode shapes of structures from eigenvalue problems.[web:1][web:10][web:11]
Electrical & control System stability and transient response from eigenvalues of state matrices.[web:1][web:7][web:12]
Markov chains Steady‑state distribution as eigenvector of the transition matrix with eigenvalue 1.[web:1][web:7][web:14]
Quantum physics Discrete energy levels as eigenvalues of Hamiltonian operators.[web:3][web:8]
